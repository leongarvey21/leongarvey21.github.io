Logo Design Exploration - Vector Branding

Overview

This project showcases a series of logo concepts created as part of a college design exercise which required me to use my initials, focused on building proficiency in Adobe Illustrator, vector workflows, and brand mark development. The set includes 9 unique logo ideas, most with two visual variants to explore contrast, colour, and composition. Each design began as a sketch and was refined through vector drawing, path editing, type manipulation, and shape-building techniques.

What I Practiced

  • Vector drawing and shape construction
  • Pen tool accuracy and path refinement
  • Type manipulation and custom lettering
  • Use of grids, balance, and negative space
  • Colour testing and contrast adjustments
  • Creating alternate versions for versatility

Design Approach

Every logo explores a different visual direction - from monogram designs to shield-style badges, retro marks, and simplified icon forms. The goal was to experiment with a wide range of styles while keeping each concept clean, scalable, and suitable for potential real-world application in signage and branding.

Tools Used:

  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Shape Builder tool
  • Pen tool
  • Pathfinder
  • Appearance and stroke settings
  • Artboard layout and variant previews

Outcome

This exercise resulted in a diverse library of vector graphics that demonstrate my technical abilitiy in Illustrator and my capability to explore multiple design styles. The final collection showcases strong versatility, attention to detail, and readiness to take on real-world branding and signage work.

Project Brief

The brief was to design a gift voucher that reflected a handmade, elegant brand identity for a local sewing business. The client requested a clean layout with classic typography and a soft green accent, suitable for both print and digital use.

Constraints: 2-Colour budget.

Delverables: Print-Ready voucher front/back + social preview.

Design Approach

Working close with the client, I refined an initial concept into a cohesive voucher design that balanced professionalism with a handmade feel. The design focused on clear typography, balanced spacing, and subtle visual details to reinforce the brand's craft-based identity.

Custom imagery inspired by sewing tools was integrated into the design to add personality without overwhelming the layout. Particular attention was given to how the voucher would be printed, ensuring consistency, legibility, and ease of reproduction across formats.

Once the main layout was finalized, I refined the design for print. This meant adjusting alignment, spacing, and sizing so the vouchers would be consistent across different formats. I kept the overall style simple, practical, and easy to reproduce while ensuring it reflected Carrie’s vision. The result was a design that not only looked professional but also captured the essence of her brand.

Final Outcome

The final design delivered a clean, print-ready voucher system that aligned with the client's brand values while remaining practical for everyday business use. The custom logo mark and restrained colour palette helped create a recognisable identity that felt personal, professional, and easy to apply across future materials.
